The problem

The people who get things done don't use apps. They answer the phone.

The office pantry, the driver, home help, grandparents. Every day we make the same short calls: "Can you bring the car?", "Set up the room by 3", "Is it done yet?". Each one needs a reminder, a follow-up, and someone to remember the answer. Alexa+ understands the request, but it can't pick up the phone. CallAssist can.

1

You ask Alexa

"Alexa, ask Raju to set up the meeting room by 3."

2

Alexa reads it back

Who, what, when. Nothing happens until you say yes.

3

CallAssist calls

It says it's an AI, explains the task, and gets a clear yes and a time.

4

It follows through

It brings questions back to you, follows up after the deadline, and tells you when it's done.

What you can say

Talk normally. Amazon Nova Pro understands.

The Alexa+ experience uses Amazon Nova Pro on Amazon Bedrock to pick the right CallAssist tool from whatever you say. Common phrases and "yes" take an instant path.

"Get me a coffee.""Could you get me a chai? I'm stuck in a meeting.""Ask Raju to set up the meeting room by 3." "Tell Grandma I'll pick her up at 5 and ask if she needs anything.""Can the driver bring the car round to the front?" "What's pending?""Any update from Raju?""Follow up with the driver.""Tell him it's room 3.""Cancel the coffee."

Safety

Enforced by the server, not the prompt

Your yes, checked

Each read-back carries a one-time token tied to exactly what was read back. No token, no call, and a retry never calls twice.

Always says it's an AI

Every call starts by saying who it's calling for and that the person can say stop at any time.

Consent on record

When, how, and the exact words agreed to. No record, no call.

Stop means never

"Don't call me again" goes on a timestamped do-not-call list immediately.

Calling hours

8 AM to 9 PM in the called person's time zone. Outside that, the call is scheduled and you're told.

Guarded inputs

What people say on the phone is treated as data, never as instructions. Rate limits and call caps on every endpoint.
