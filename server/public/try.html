Try CallAssist in 3 minutes

You are Madhu. Talk to Alexa+, answer the phone next to it, and watch the MCP log. Tap a green line to say it, or type anything in your own words. Only these demo phones ring: no real numbers are called from this page.

1Give someone a task

  1. Say the line below. Alexa reads it back and waits.
  2. Say yes. Raju's phone rings on the right.
  3. Answer it and tap a reply, like "Okay, 20 minutes".
  4. Alexa tells you Raju is on it.

2Find a time to meet

  1. Amazon Nova Pro understands it: CallAssist asks Neeta when she's free.
  2. Say yes, answer Neeta's phone, and tell her a time, like "11:30 works".
  3. Alexa brings her answer back.

3Send a message and get a reply

  1. Say yes. Grandma's phone rings.
  2. Answer and reply, like "Yes, some milk please".
  3. Alexa brings her answer back to you.

4Check on everything

First time? Tap Tap to start on the Echo Show and Link account (one tap, like enabling a skill). Turn your sound on to hear Alexa and the call.
